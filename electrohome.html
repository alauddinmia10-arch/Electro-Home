YongFa Inverter with Charger SC 1000W

The Yongfa SC-1000 is a high-performance 1000W solar inverter designed for reliable power conversion from 12V DC to 220V AC. Engineered with integrated IPS functionalities and a smart battery charger (max 10A), this inverter is ideal for homes, small offices, and emergency backup systems. With advanced protection mechanisms and aerospace-grade technology, it ensures stability, efficiency, and long-term durability. 

Key Features 

  • High conversion efficiency with smart functionality
  • IPS dual operation capability
  • Built-in battery charger with auto cut-off (max 10A)
  • 5V 1A USB port for mobile device charging
  • Smart cooling system with MCU control for fan and working status
  • Universal AC socket compatible with various plug types
  • Modified sine wave output for standard electrical appliances
  • Multiple safety protections – overload, short circuit, high temp, reverse polarity, etc.
